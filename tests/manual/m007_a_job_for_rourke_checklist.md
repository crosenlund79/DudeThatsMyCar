# Manual playtest checklist — Milestone 007: A Job for Rourke

Play `src/main/main.tscn` with keyboard, mouse and headphones. Tick each item; note problems in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (not as tasks). To see the job from the start, start a new game (delete `user://chicago1932_save.json`); a game saved before M007 starts with the job not yet offered.

## The offer
- [ ] At Rourke's table, "You said you had work, Mr. Rourke?" is among the answers (in place of "How's business?"). His line is short and in character; the small direction beside his name ("leaning in, low") reads as a performance note, not as clutter.
- [ ] "Give me a night or two": he lets it go tonight and brings it up again the next night; put off three times, he gives it to someone else.
- [ ] "Not my kind of work": he is cool about it, not hostile; business with him goes on as before.
- [ ] "I'll handle it": a notice says what to do next (ask Mr. Lindqvist).

## Finding out
- [ ] Mr. Lindqvist: "Rourke says somebody's been squeezing you" - he names Lefty Moran and where he stands; nervous, believable.
- [ ] Eddie: "Know anything about Lefty Moran?" - a useful tip, in Eddie's voice.
- [ ] M: under the key "ROURKE'S JOB" says what next in a few words; a thin copper ring circles the place it concerns (the hardware store, then Lefty's corner, then the Copper Owl); Lefty's corner is marked only once you know his name. No route, no arrows, nothing on the street itself.

## Lefty Moran
- [ ] Before the job, or before Lindqvist names him, Lefty is the M006 Lefty ("Keep walking, pal").
- [ ] Once you know: "Mercer. Rourke's errand boy..." with talk, threaten and fight; he never starts a fight himself.
- [ ] Talk: he explains himself; "Keep what you took. Just stop." settles it; asking for Rourke's money back gets laughed off, and you can try something else.
- [ ] Threaten with your hands in your pockets: he calls the bluff; you cannot try it again tonight. With the gun out (or after Eddie's tip) he folds and hands over twenty dollars.
- [ ] Shoot him: the M006 rules (he shoots back, the cop may hear). Wounded, he will not talk any more; killed, the job is settled the hard way.

## The fistfight
- [ ] "Put 'em up, Lefty" then "(Raise your fists.)" (or E): the brass dial appears over the street above the two men; the game does not zoom or change screen; the clock, purse and minimap stay readable.
- [ ] The dial looks of its period (dark brass and copper, aged cream markings, worn metal) and original; the needle and the lit zone are easy to read at a glance.
- [ ] Space while the needle is in the zone lands a punch (gold flash, "LANDED", a thud; Lefty recoils); missing or pressing early takes one (red flash, "TAKEN", Danny recoils). The five pips show the score.
- [ ] The zone moves from check to check and the needle's pace varies a little; it is approachable, not frantic. Eddie's tip makes it noticeably easier.
- [ ] During the fight W A S D, clicks, Space as handbrake, M and C do nothing else; nobody else can start a fight; afterwards Danny walks normally at once.
- [ ] Three or more landed: Lefty gives up from the ground and hands over twenty dollars. Fewer: he gloats, Danny has a black eye (C shows the head as wounded), the job stays open; talk is still there, a threat is harder, and he will not fight again tonight. Nobody dies.

## Telling Rourke
- [ ] "About Lindqvist's, Mr. Rourke...": the truth, the truth with Lefty's twenty handed over, the truth keeping it quiet, or "It's handled" when it is not.
- [ ] His reaction differs: a deal (sour, then a shrug), leaned on or beaten (pleased), a killing (uneasy); never hostile for the way you chose.
- [ ] His reward comes out of what you have handed him; if his purse is empty he takes it off your debt or pays it with your next envelope (he says so).
- [ ] Give Lindqvist his twenty back: he is moved; Rourke remarks on it if you tell him.
- [ ] A lie (Lefty's money kept, or "It's handled") comes home about three nights later: Rourke greets you with it, wants the money, trusts you less; after "It's handled" he still wants the job done.
- [ ] The next evenings, Augie's "news" passes on how the ward heard it was settled.
