# Manual playtest checklist — Milestone 008: Beyond Riverside Ward

Play `src/main/main.tscn` with keyboard, mouse and headphones, ideally on a real graphics card. Tick each item; note problems in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (not as tasks).

## Armed police
- [ ] Fire into the air near the beat cop: he blows his whistle and comes after you, but does not draw.
- [ ] Shoot a passer-by (or Lefty) where the beat cop can see it: "The copper draws his revolver!", the HUD line turns to "THE COPPER IS SHOOTING AT YOU", he stops at a gunfight's distance and fires every couple of seconds after a moment to aim; his shots flash and sound like gunfire.
- [ ] His rounds hurt Danny where they land (C shows it); standing in the open long enough, Danny goes down and comes to on the Doc's table, and the copper stands down.
- [ ] Behind a building he never fires; with a parked car between you he holds his fire and steps aside for a clear line.
- [ ] Driving away under fire, a round now and then goes through the car to Danny - dangerous, not hopeless. He loses you as before ("You lost him") and puts the revolver away.
- [ ] Passers-by never draw; they run, as before. Lefty still shoots back only when provoked.
- [ ] It feels dangerous but playable: not too accurate, not too many.

## The Riverfront Yards (south)
- [ ] From the flat, drive south down Kessler or Wharf Street past Pruitt Street: the yards are simply there as you arrive - no pause, no pop-in, no loading screen.
- [ ] Front Street: warehouses and works instead of shops; the Great Lakes Tanning Co. with its smoking stack; North Branch Freight's loading yard with parked trucks; Brennan Coal & Ice on its dock by the river; Dutch's Night Lunch lit for the night shift.
- [ ] Foundry Street, the lane behind the works, the board-fenced sidings with boxcars behind the gate on Wharf Street, the railroad embankment closing the south.
- [ ] Dimmer, sparser lamps; a couple of workmen and one car about; a beat cop on Front Street.
- [ ] The river carries on from the ward, its far bank still out of reach.

## Halloran Square (west)
- [ ] Drive west along Marlowe Avenue past Dorsey Street: the streetcar tracks carry on into Halloran Square with no stop.
- [ ] Buff-brick and greystone flats with many warm windows, corner shops with awnings (Kowalski Bakery, Halloran Drugs), the Bijou picture house with its own lit canopy, St. Brendan's church on the corner, the square with its plane trees, benches and lamps.
- [ ] A few more people about than in the yards; quieter than the Copper Owl's block; its own beat cop by the square.
- [ ] Calloway Street is still barricaded at the ward's west end; Dorsey Street at its south end.

## Seamless travel
- [ ] Walk across a border as well as drive: nothing changes but the district name in the top-left corner.
- [ ] Drive from the yards back to the ward and on into Halloran Square: no stutter you can feel at any border.
- [ ] Leave your sedan in the yards, go to Halloran Square, come back: the sedan is where you left it, and no car has been doubled.
- [ ] Get chased by a beat cop to his district's edge: he stops there and gives up as usual.

## The city map (M)
- [ ] M opens the map on you; drag with the mouse to look round, the wheel zooms in and out (not past the whole city, not closer than street level), R or the button takes you back to yourself; M or Esc closes it.
- [ ] All three districts in their places, with dashed borders and their names, the streets, the river and bridges, the landmarks and the badges of the places you can walk into.
- [ ] You can look at the yards or the square without going there.
- [ ] Labels and badges readable at 1280x720.
- [ ] The small map in the corner runs on across a border and shows the other districts' beat cops.

## Saving, sleeping and carrying on
- [ ] Save and quit in the yards or the square: the game loads at home in the ward as always, and the other districts appear when you go there.
- [ ] Sleep: the next evening every district is as it starts a night (cars back with their owners, a new man on every beat).
- [ ] Back in the ward: the Copper Owl, Rourke's envelope and job, the Bird, the vet and Lefty all as before.

## Performance
- [ ] On your own machine: no hitch when a district appears or goes; steady frame rate at the borders and with two districts loaded.
