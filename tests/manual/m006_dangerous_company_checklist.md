# Manual playtest checklist — Milestone 006: Dangerous Company

Play `src/main/main.tscn` with keyboard, mouse and headphones. Tick each item; note problems in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (not as tasks). To hear the first meetings, start a new game (delete `user://chicago1932_save.json`); a game saved before M006 counts Augie, Rourke and Eddie as already met.

## Voices
- [ ] Talk to Augie for the first time: he greets you in his own voice; the page shows his words; then his usual page. The second time: no greeting.
- [ ] Rourke's first greeting ("Look what the wind dragged back into Chicago...") plays once; it fits a man back after years away.
- [ ] Eddie greets you; "Let's see what you've got" plays his sales line, after his greeting if it is still going (never both at once).
- [ ] While anyone speaks the music and street step back, and come back up after. E walks away and the voice fades out at once.
- [ ] Quit and reload: nobody greets you a second time.
- [ ] Listening: the voices are clear, at an even level with each other and with Danny's monologue; nothing distorts.

## The Bird
- [ ] In the Copper Owl she stands at her microphone in front of the piano, clearly visible in her spotlight, swaying gently to the song and changing gestures now and then.
- [ ] E at her microphone ("Interrupt the Bird"): a record scratch, the song stops, she complains ("Ever heard of waiting till a lady finishes her song...").
- [ ] The first time, "Do I know you?" brings her introduction - after the complaint, never over it.
- [ ] Walk away: a moment later the song starts again and she sings; no double music, no second scratch if you go back during the pause.
- [ ] It is short and charming, not a relationship system.

## Walking
- [ ] Danny's feet step at a walking pace (about two steps a second), not running; standing still and aiming, he does not step.

## Lefty Moran and wounds
- [ ] Outside Doyle's Garage (Pruitt Street) a gangster in a camel coat and white fedora loiters; walking past or talking to him is safe.
- [ ] Shoot at him (or past him): he draws and fires back at a distance, follows you, and you can get away; badly hurt he runs.
- [ ] Hits show (a red flush); people limp; struck down, they fall and lie still with a dark patch beneath (no gore).
- [ ] Shot down, Lefty stays down; the next night his body is gone and he never comes back (also after quitting and reloading).
- [ ] Shoot a passer-by, a patron or the beat cop: they can die; the next evening there are passers-by, patrons and a cop again.
- [ ] Shoot Augie (or another of the ward's own people): he goes down and is carried off; nobody to talk to there tonight; back the next evening; Rourke and the ward remember.
- [ ] Get badly hurt: you come to on Doc Pettibone's table, a few dollars poorer.
