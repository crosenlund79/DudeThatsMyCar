# Manual playtest checklist — Milestone 003: A Night's Work

Play `src/main/main.tscn` with keyboard and mouse (delete `user://chicago1932_save.json` first for a fresh start). Tick each item; note problems in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (not as tasks).

## Driving like a road car (Sedan, Roadster, the squad car)
- [ ] Standing still, A / D turns the front wheels (visible at the fenders) and the car does not move or rotate.
- [ ] Pulling away is weighty; lifting off, the car rolls on rather than braking; S brakes firmly but not with a jolt, then reverses.
- [ ] Turning feels like a car: the rear follows the front round a believable circle; no spinning on the spot, no snap changes of direction.
- [ ] Too fast for a corner, the car runs wide instead of whipping round; slowing for the corner fixes it. Nothing about it feels like a racer.
- [ ] Nosing into the back alleys and turning at the crossings is easy at a sensible speed; reversing out of an alley goes straight.
- [ ] A shallow scrape along a wall keeps you moving and turns the nose along the wall without a jerk; head-on stops you.
- [ ] The handbrake still swings the tail round with a squeal for a quick turn.

## Rooms you can walk into
- [ ] Each door (warm light on the pavement) lets you in; the roof and walls fade away and the lit room shows from above; they come back as you leave.
- [ ] The Copper Owl: bar and back bar, Augie behind it, guests at the tables, the baby grand; walls, bar and tables are solid; doors swing.
- [ ] Sal's, Novak's, Lindqvist's with Eddie's back room, and your flat all read as period rooms; the light inside is warm, outside stays night.
- [ ] No car fits through a door.

## A night's work
- [ ] Sal sells you rye; the money and the bottles show under the place name; he runs out after six.
- [ ] Augie buys it for more; he takes six a night. His protection: asking and leaning give different money and a different mood (his rye price).
- [ ] Novak pays once a night; lean on him, step outside, and the copper comes looking.
- [ ] Eddie sells a box of rounds; the gun readout shows the spare rounds; an empty gun only clicks.
- [ ] Characters sound like 1932 Chicago; answers are short, numbered, and E walks away.
- [ ] Repeating a deal never pays twice in a night.

## The night and sleeping
- [ ] The clock at the top starts at 7:30 PM and runs; from 4 AM you are told to get home (GET HOME TO BED), at dawn again; nothing forces you.
- [ ] At your bed: sleep through the day. The next night starts at home at 7:30 PM, money and bottles kept, deals fresh, cars back at their curbs, the copper on his round.
- [ ] Quit and restart: money, bottles and rounds are still there.

## The streets
- [ ] A handful of people stroll the sidewalks of Marlowe Avenue and Kessler Street; a few cars with headlights go round the blocks on the right.
- [ ] Cars slow for corners, wait for each other at a crossing and stop for you standing in the road, then drive on.
- [ ] People step out of the way of a car coming at them and run from gunfire.
- [ ] Late at night there are fewer of them; nobody pops in or out where you can see.
- [ ] The squad car (POLICE on the roof) waits in the alley behind Novak's.
- [ ] Frame rate stays smooth on your machine on Marlowe Avenue with traffic and inside the Copper Owl.
