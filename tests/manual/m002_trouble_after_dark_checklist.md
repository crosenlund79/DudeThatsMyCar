# Manual playtest checklist — Milestone 002: Trouble After Dark

Play `src/main/main.tscn` with keyboard and mouse. Tick each item; note problems in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (not as tasks).

## Driving (Sedan and Roadster)
- [ ] Pulling away feels weighty: the car gathers speed over a second or two, never sluggish; the Roadster is clearly quicker.
- [ ] Steering builds as you hold A / D and straightens smoothly; no snapping to a new direction.
- [ ] At walking pace the car turns along a tight, predictable circle: easy to nose into an alley or a parking space.
- [ ] At speed it is steadier; corners at Marlowe / Kessler can be taken cleanly at a sensible speed.
- [ ] Scraping a wall at a shallow angle keeps most of the speed and turns the car along the wall; a head-on hit stops it. Never through a wall.
- [ ] Reverse (S from a standstill), braking and the handbrake still feel intuitive; reversing out of an alley is easy.

## The handgun
- [ ] Left click draws the .45 (arm out, pistol visible) and fires towards the mouse, whichever way you walk.
- [ ] Muzzle flash lights the street for an instant; the shot sounds like a pistol in a brick street.
- [ ] Bullets streak, stop at walls (brick dust, a spark, a thud) and at cars (sparks, a clank); none pass through a building.
- [ ] Seven shots, then it reloads by itself (sound, "reloading..." on the HUD); rapid clicks are not lost.
- [ ] The gun goes away after a quiet while and when you get into a car; no shooting from the wheel.

## The beat cop
- [ ] From the start you can see him on his short round by the newsstand and his call box, torch in hand.
- [ ] Fire nearby: he freezes, blows his whistle, "!" over his cap, "A COPPER IS ON YOUR TAIL" at the top; then he runs at you.
- [ ] Fire far away (e.g. Pruitt Street): he does not react.
- [ ] He goes round buildings (try shooting in the alley behind the Copper Owl) and does not get stuck for long on cars or lamp posts.
- [ ] Run for the roadster, drive off down Marlowe Avenue, turn into an alley: he loses you, searches ("?", "THE COPPER IS LOOKING FOR YOU"), gives up ("You lost him.") and walks back to his round.
- [ ] Slip away on foot round a corner or through a gangway: the same.
- [ ] He never seems to know where you are while he cannot see you.

## The night
- [ ] The district still looks as in M001: lamps, lit windows, rooftops, wet streets, the river. Nothing redesigned.
- [ ] The frame rate is smooth on your machine during a chase (lamps, torch, muzzle flash).
