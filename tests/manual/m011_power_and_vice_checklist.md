## The gaming room
- [ ] In the Copper Owl the west door still says "Through to The Copper Owl's Back Room"; the card room is as it was (the table, the three regulars, the green lamp). Its west wall has a door: "Through to The Copper Owl's Gaming Room".
- [ ] The gaming room feels like an illicit back room, not a casino: dark boards, a long shaded lamp over the wheel, three worn machines along the back wall, small tables with drinks and ashtrays, prints and a clock on the walls, a sideboard, smoke; the croupier (green eyeshade) and a few players. The roof opens over it as you come in and closes over the card room behind you.
- [ ] You can walk between the wheel's rail and each machine round the furniture and the people; the tables and machines are solid. E at the rail plays the wheel, E at a machine plays that machine, and leaving puts you back in the room where you stood.

## The card game (preserved)

## The wheel
- [ ] The wheel's numbers run 0, 32, 15, 19, 4, 21 ... clockwise; red and black alternate; one green zero.
- [ ] 1-4 (or the mouse wheel) picks a chip; a click on a number, a line, a crossing, the edge under a column or between two, or an outside box puts it there; what it covers lights up with what it pays; a right click takes one back; Backspace clears; R bets the same again. You cannot bet money you do not have, more than the limits, or anything while it spins.
- [ ] Space spins: the head turns, the ball runs round the other way, drops, rattles over the frets and settles; the number it settles in is the one marked (the brass dolly), lit on the layout, read out at the bottom, added to the last numbers; winning chips glow, the others are raked; the purse is right to the cent (try a straight number, a split, a corner, red, a dozen; and a zero if you are patient).
- [ ] Esc mid-spin: you get up at once and the spin is settled (no second payment later). The wheel in the room turns while it spins and now and then for the other players.
- [ ] Sounds: the spin and the ball's run, its drop and its knock into the pocket, chips on the felt, the croupier's bell, the payout or the rake - no speech.

## The machines
- [ ] Three different machines (the Owl, the Bell, the Star), each with its own front and paytable; the Star takes a dime.
- [ ] Space pulls the lever (it comes down and springs back); the reels run and stop left, middle, right, each with a clunk; the line shown is what is paid by the table on the front (its line lit), coins drop into the cup with a clink each, the bell for a good win. Holding Space pulls once. No sign of anything modern: no bonus rounds, free spins, flashing jackpots.
- [ ] No nickel, no pull. Esc mid-pull pays the pull at once. The reels' whirr stops with the last reel and never doubles.
