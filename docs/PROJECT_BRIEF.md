# Project Brief — Single design and task handoff record

This file **records** decisions and approvals for *Chicago, 1932*; it never grants them. Only the user can approve scope, in their message in the current conversation. **Undecided = ask, don't choose.** The game was converted from a copy of HyperCars; the HyperCars record is archived in `docs/archive/hypercars/PROJECT_BRIEF.md`.

## Game decisions (user-confirmed only)

- **Game title:** *Chicago, 1932* — working title, placeholder (GDD). Repository `DudeThatsMyCar`, an independent game; the original HyperCars repository is never modified from here (user, 2026-11-17).
- **Godot version:** 4.7.x — developed and tested with 4.7.2-stable, GL Compatibility renderer (carried over from HyperCars).
- **Premise / direction:** a top-down, GTA-like gangster sandbox in Prohibition-era Chicago, around 1932, with an original identity (user, kickoff 2026-11-17; `docs/chicago_1932_game_design_document.md` §2). The user's vision: a freely explorable city (streets, intersections, alleys, traffic, pedestrians); responsive 1930s vehicle handling; gangsters, police, rival gangs, Tommy guns, car chases; bootlegging, robberies, rackets, speakeasies, criminal jobs; walking, getting in and out of vehicles, basic interactions; police attention, money, reputation, consequences; a living city with dark humour and period atmosphere; fun even when just driving around. *This is direction, not approval: every feature needs its own approved objective.* The GDD's other sections are proposals.
- **Perspective / art direction:** true top-down gameplay camera with enough depth to show building height and facades, never an extreme perspective that hides the roads (user, M001). Hand-painted, slightly whimsical HyperCars visual identity with a convincing 1930s urban atmosphere (user, M001; HyperCars approval 2026-11-14). Style reference: the user's drawings in `docs/art_refrence/` — study their characteristics only (soft brushwork, organic outlines, imperfect shapes, soft shading, colour, proportions); never recreate the depicted objects. `template_` graphics must be proper, coherent art, not debug shapes, consistent so the user's art can replace them one at a time through the `custom/` override contract, including next to an exported Windows/Linux game without rebuilding.
- **The nighttime henchman (user, M002, approved direction):** the protagonist is a henchman who works through the night and sleeps by day. The whole playable game takes place at night for now — no daytime rendering, no day / night cycle, no sleeping mechanics yet. The long-term atmosphere is "dark and darker": lively lit nightlife, quiet midnight streets, threatening late-night alleys. Riverside Ward's night look is the visual foundation; do not redesign the neighbourhood or regenerate its artwork unnecessarily.
- **Controls (user, M001, M002):** on foot — WASD move, mouse aims independently, left click shoots, E interact / get into a car. Driving — W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A / D steer, Space handbrake, E get out.
- **Driving (user, M001, M002):** satisfying, accessible city driving, not a simulator: the HyperCars core kept, with weight (gradual acceleration), steering that builds rather than snaps, low-speed precision for streets, alleys and parking, glancing wall contact that slides with appropriate momentum (never through solid objects), intuitive reverse / braking / handbrake, meaningful Sedan / Roadster differences; targeted, configurable changes rather than a new model. Exits never into walls or inaccessible places.
- **Handgun (user, M002):** one simple period handgun: visible gun and muzzle flash, bullets with sensible speed, range and collision, a period gunshot sound, simple ammunition without an inventory, shots blocked by buildings and solid obstacles, basic impact feedback. No other weapons, shops or upgrades yet.
- **Police (user, M002, experimental):** one visible officer who idles or patrols near a street corner, reacts visibly to nearby gunfire and pursues, navigates round buildings, can be escaped on foot or by car, does not know where the player is forever, gives up after the player stays undetected a while and returns to his patrol. Not yet: wanted levels, reinforcements, police cars, arrests, police gunfights, citywide searches.
- **Atmosphere (user, M001):** start at night; dense brick buildings with flat roofs and recognisable rooftop details, fire escapes, narrow spaces between buildings, lit windows, intersections, sidewalks, curbs, back alleys, period street lamps on wet reflective streets, subtle smoke / steam / shadows; a speakeasy / jazz club, a garage and a warehouse; a few parked 1930s cars; handcrafted, intentionally designed, part of a larger neighbourhood rather than an enclosed course.
- **Not yet (user, M002):** pedestrian crowds, civilian traffic, police cars, more weapons, full combat AI, criminal factions, missions, money, reputation, inventories, interiors, expanded districts, daily schedules.
- **Retired:** no playable racing mode (user, M001). The conversion needed no backup tag (user, M001); HyperCars stays in its own repository and in this repository's history (commit `c6f7511`).
- **Testing framework:** GUT 9.7.1 (vendored in `addons/gut/`). From the project root: `godot --headless --path . --import` once (and after adding a `class_name` script), then `godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 -s addons/gut/gut_cmdln.gd` (config `.gutconfig.json`; engine errors and `push_error` fail tests). Manual checklists: `tests/manual/m001_city_checklist.md`, `tests/manual/m002_trouble_after_dark_checklist.md`. GUT skips a test file that fails to parse and still reports success: check that the script count matches. Screenshots: `xvfb-run -a godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 -s tests/support/screenshot_tour.gd -- <dir>`.
- **Target platforms:** Undecided (runtime asset overrides cover Windows/Linux only; other platforms need an explicit decision).
- **Asset override notes:** contract in `src/assets/asset_catalog.gd`, generated list with sizes and override paths in `docs/ASSET_SPECS.md`, rules in `assets/README.md`. Kinds: sprites (scaled to a display size), a sprite sheet (the gangster: 5 frames of 64×64 in a row, facing right), seamless tiles and strips, facades (seamless walls, top half an upper storey, bottom half the ground storey; one repeat = 128 world px) and `_lit` light images (night window light, additive). Template art is generated by `tools/template_art/` and the M002 sound templates by `tools/template_audio/` (Python, PIL + numpy; Godot-ignored).

## Existing modules / boundaries (M001, M002)

- `src/driving/` — `CarModel` (pure deterministic model from HyperCars: momentum, grip slide, wall and car contacts, contact yaw; M001 adds throttle / brake-then-reverse / handbrake / speed-scaled steering / parked state; M002 adds engine build-up and fade, a steering wheel with rise / return times, the tightest turning circle at low speed, steadier steering at speed and wall friction with nose alignment — every M002 parameter defaults to the M001 behaviour), `DrivingParams`, `Car` (physics body; parked cars can be shoved and stop; nudged by bullets), `VehicleType` + `vehicles/sedan.tres`, `vehicles/coupe.tres`.
- `src/weapons/` — `GunRules` (`default_pistol.tres`, provisional), `Gun` (pure handgun state: draw, one shot per click, reload, holster), `BulletWorld` (bullets ray-checked every tick against buildings, cars and people; `hit_by_bullet` on what they strike).
- `src/police/` — `PoliceRules` (`default_police.tres`, provisional), `OfficerBrain` (pure state machine: patrol, alerted, chase, search, return), `PoliceOfficer` (a `Walker` body with torch, badges and whistle, following `CityNavigator` routes). The player's `fired` signal is wired to the officer in `src/main/`.
- `src/city/` — `DistrictLayout` (pure data and rules, incl. the police beat), `RiversideWard` (the hand-made district), `CityNavigator` (walking routes on Godot's `AStarGrid2D`), `City` (builds ground, curbs, tracks, river, solid footprints, buildings, street furniture, lamps; `update_view` every frame), `BuildingView` (perspective walls by storey, window light, roofs, parapets, fire escapes, rooftop furniture and signs), `Perspective` (projection maths), `NightAtmosphere` (night tint, moonlight on roofs, steam, chimney smoke, haze, vignette), `DrawLayer`, `Layers` (physics layers, light masks, draw order).
- `src/actors/` — `WalkerModel` (pure on-foot movement), `Walker` (a person's body — the gangster, the officer: collision, walk cycle, faces the aim, armed sheet and pistol when a gun is drawn).
- `src/player/` — `CityInput` (actions, keyboard + mouse), `PlayerCommand`, `PlayerController` (walking ⇄ driving, enter / exit, shooting on foot), `ExitFinder` (exit spots).
- `src/camera/` — `CityCamera` (smooth follow, look-ahead, zoom by mode and speed, eye height for the perspective).
- `src/presentation/` — `VehicleLights`, `VehicleAudio` (HyperCars engine sound model), `GunfireView` (muzzle flash, tracers, impacts, gun sounds), `SoftShadow`, `LightTextures`. Presentation only reads state.
- `src/ui/` — `Hud` (location, prompt, car and speed, rounds, pursuit line, notices, controls card, aim reticle, pause).
- `src/assets/` — `AssetResolver` (unchanged override / fallback resolver), `AssetCatalog`. `src/audio/` — `AudioSettings`, `EngineSoundParams` (retained). `src/damage/` — `DamageRules`, `DamageWorld` (retained pure condition model, not yet wired in).
- `src/main/` — `main.tscn` composition root (the run scene); no Autoloads.
- Retired HyperCars systems that may be worth reviving later are in commit `c6f7511`: music player with crossfade loops, voice director, remappable controls and settings / controls screens, combat world and weapon rules, AI drivers (racing and arena pursuit), network play, lighting pilot, weather view, animated wheels, achievements.
